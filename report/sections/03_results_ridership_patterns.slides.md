# Results: Ridership Patterns

`fig03_boardings_by_hour.png`

- Weekday peaks: **9:00 (195.9)** and **16:00 (175.9)**, matching the commute
- Weekend peak: **15:00 (124.6)**, 36% lower
- **2,514** boardings per weekday vs **1,728** per weekend day (+45%)

`fig04_daily_boardings.png`

- Busiest month: **October** (3,025 per weekday)
- **Thanksgiving:** 1,146 boardings, less than half a normal weekday
- **Christmas Day:** 871, the lowest day overall

`fig05_headway_distribution.png`

- Median headway **11.0 min** (scheduled 10)
- **5.7%** bunched (<3 min), **10.3%** long gaps (>20 min)
- About **1 in 6** arrivals is bunched or a long gap
