# Results: where and when passengers board

*Owner: Rence · Figure: `figures/fig02_boardings_heatmap.png`*

- Boardings are concentrated at a few stops. **Stop 1** (the first stop) is the busiest, with up to about 40 boardings per weekday in a single hour around 7:00–10:00. **Stops 5 and 19** are the next busiest. Over a whole weekday, stop 1 averages about 442 boardings, stop 5 about 329 and stop 19 about 204.
- Stop 5 is busiest in the morning (6:00–9:00). Stop 19 is busiest in the afternoon (15:00–17:00), which suggests commuters travelling out in the morning and back in the afternoon.
- Apart from stops 22 and 24, the stops after stop 20 have few boardings at any hour, because passengers are mostly getting off near the end of the route.
- Demand is uneven across both stops and hours. A bus reaching a busy stop at a busy hour has many more people to serve, and the hypothesis results test whether that is tied to bunching.
